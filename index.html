What this is

This is a three-step exploratory and reflective process centered on your lived experience

In the first conversation, you revisit significant moments and put into words what you knew but had never articulated. The guided dialogue is valuable in its own right, because connections emerge between episodes that seemed separate.

The written synthesis you receive, your livret, gives you concrete food for thought, to make sense of your path, professional, personal, or both, and to build on. As you see yourself and your life from a different perspective, meaning begins to take shape, with first directions to explore.

The follow-up conversation draws on your livret to clarify your current situation and what is at stake. Relying on the value of your experience and the logic and purpose of your actions and decisions, now made explicit, you can consider a way that includes what matters to you.

Useful when

When periods of transition, uncertainty, or standstill call for deeper reflection and clarity

…your situation no longer works for you, and you can't clearly express, or define, what matters to you, as a foundation for figuring out what's next.

…you don't know how to translate your experience into core expertise, and you don't fully appreciate the patterns that drive your achievements.

…you hold yourself back from aiming higher, further, or elsewhere, because you don't lean on what you have done, don't see the extent of what you know, or don't understand what makes your impact.

…a transition is coming, or you are already at a turning point, and you don't know where to start, or how to guide yourself through it.

How it works

Through a research-based reflective interview and in-depth exploration

First conversation

01 about 90 minutes

Explore significant lived moments and situations

These episodes are worked through one at a time, in detail, with questions that bring out their implicit significance.

Reveal recurring patterns, tensions, and dynamics

A trained reading identifies the expertise at work and the core values across these episodes.

Consider how they continue to shape decisions and action

Through dialogue, the tacit logic underlying how you think and how you act comes into view, as the same mechanism keeps showing up.

Your livret

02

Receive a written synthesis to work from, with new angles to take forward

The livret gathers the whole analysis into one document, a reference kept and returned to long after the interview.

Follow-up conversation

03 about an hour

Deepen your understanding and act on it

In conversation, the livret's contribution is put to use for what comes next, opening real possibilities.

What emerges

A coherent picture, across your professional and personal journey, and meaning from your experience.

You put words to the unique expertise and patterns at work in your decisions, your intuitions, and situations that occur.

You gain clearer direction and firmer ground for what you will accomplish from here: stepping into a new role, weighing a founder choice, or seizing an opportunity.

You see what is distinctly yours in how you operate, lead, and perform, and use this knowledge as a strategic resource for the next step or leadership moments.
